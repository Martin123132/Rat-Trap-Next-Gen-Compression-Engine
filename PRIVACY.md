# Rat-Trap Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 July 2026

Rat-Trap is provided by **TWO HANDS NETWORK LTD** ("Two Hands Network",
"we", "us", or "our"). This policy applies to the Rat-Trap Windows desktop
application.

## Short version

Rat-Trap is a local file application. It does not require an account and does
not include advertising, analytics, telemetry, or an automatic cloud service.
The application does not automatically transmit the files, passwords,
recovery keys, search queries, archive contents, or diagnostic information
that it processes to Two Hands Network or a Rat-Trap-operated online service.

Rat-Trap necessarily accesses files and folders that the user explicitly
selects. Those files may contain personal information. Processing and storage
remain on the user's computer or on a storage location chosen by the user.

## Information Rat-Trap accesses

When the user chooses a file, folder, archive, repository, or destination,
Rat-Trap may access:

- file and folder names, paths, sizes, timestamps, and contents;
- checksums and other technical metadata used to verify stored data;
- text and structured records in supported files when the user creates or
  searches a Queryable Vault;
- archive and repository metadata, snapshot labels, and file-history indexes;
- passwords entered to protect or unlock an archive or Sealed File; and
- recovery-key files selected or created by the user.

Rat-Trap uses this information only to perform the operation requested by the
user, such as compression, backup, encryption, verification, search, export,
or restoration.

## Local storage and output

Archives, repositories, Sealed Files, recovery keys, mapping reports, exports,
and restored files are written to locations selected by the user. Search and
structured-data indexes are stored inside the applicable local Rat-Trap Vault.
The application does not copy these outputs to Two Hands Network or to an
automatic online service.

Queryable Vaults may contain indexed copies of supported text or structured
data. A live Rat-Trap repository is not encrypted merely because it was
created by Rat-Trap. Users should apply appropriate device, drive, account,
and physical security.

Uninstalling Rat-Trap does not automatically delete user-created archives,
repositories, recovery keys, reports, exports, or restored files. The user
controls their retention and deletion.

## Passwords, recovery keys, and temporary plaintext

Passwords and recovery-key contents are not transmitted to Two Hands Network.
Rat-Trap uses them locally to derive or recover the cryptographic keys needed
for the requested operation.

Some protection, export, and restoration operations require temporary
plaintext on the local device. Rat-Trap warns before operations with this
boundary and attempts to remove temporary working data when the operation
finishes. Normal deletion is not guaranteed to be secure erasure, and copies
may remain in operating-system caches, backups, storage snapshots, or
previously written destinations.

A recovery-key file can unlock its matching protected data. It should be kept
separately from that data and disclosed only to people who are authorised to
read it. Optional mapping reports can reveal original filenames and should be
protected accordingly.

## Network activity and sharing

The Microsoft Store edition of Rat-Trap does not require a Rat-Trap server,
user account, or internet connection for its file operations. It does not
automatically upload user content, contact lists, usage history, crash reports,
or cryptographic material.

Microsoft Store and Windows may process installation, update, licensing, and
Store diagnostic information under Microsoft's own terms and privacy
statements. That processing is not performed by Rat-Trap.

Users can choose to move, email, upload, or otherwise share Rat-Trap outputs.
Any such sharing is controlled by the user and by the other service they
choose, not by Rat-Trap. If the user chooses a network drive or other remote
storage location as a source or destination, Windows and that storage provider
will transfer and process the selected data under the user's direction.

## Support communications

Rat-Trap does not send support information automatically. If a person contacts
Two Hands Network for support or licensing, we receive the information they
voluntarily provide, such as their email address, message, application version,
and any diagnostic material they choose to attach. We use that information to
answer the request, administer the relevant relationship, protect the product,
and meet legal obligations.

Support information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for
those purposes. People should not send passwords, recovery keys, confidential
archives, or unnecessary personal information in a support request.

## Disclosure

Rat-Trap does not sell personal information. Two Hands Network may disclose
information received through a voluntary support or licensing communication
to service providers that operate the relevant email or business systems, or
when disclosure is required by law. We do not receive the contents processed
locally by Rat-Trap unless the user separately and deliberately sends them.

## Security and user choices

Rat-Trap includes integrity checks and optional authenticated encryption for
supported portable formats. No software or storage system can guarantee
absolute security. Users choose:

- which inputs Rat-Trap may access;
- where outputs and recovery files are stored;
- whether optional reports or exports are created;
- whether protected material is restored to plaintext; and
- whether any output or support information is shared with another person or
  service.

## Access, correction, deletion, and complaints

Two Hands Network does not receive or control the files processed locally by
Rat-Trap, so it cannot access, correct, export, or delete those local files on
the user's behalf. Those choices remain with the user and the owner of the
selected storage.

Subject to applicable law, a person may ask us for access to, correction of,
or deletion of personal information that they voluntarily supplied in a
support or licensing communication. They may also ask us to restrict or object
to certain processing, or request a portable copy where that right applies.
These rights are not absolute, and we may need to verify the requester's
identity or retain information when legally required.

We limit access to voluntary support and licensing information to people and
service providers that need it for the purposes described in this policy. We
use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to that
information. Email and internet transmission cannot be guaranteed completely
secure, so sensitive files, passwords, and recovery keys should never be sent
to us.

Requests can be sent to the contact address below. A person in the United
Kingdom may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office or another
applicable data-protection authority.

## Children

Rat-Trap is a general-purpose file utility and is not directed specifically at
children. It does not knowingly operate an online service that collects
children's personal information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Rat-Trap's functionality or legal obligations
change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
