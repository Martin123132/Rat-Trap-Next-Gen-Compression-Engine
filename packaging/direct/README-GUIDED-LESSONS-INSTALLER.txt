UPGRADE AND OPTIONAL INTEGRATION

Wait for any operation to finish, then close the app before running Setup.
Setup does not kill a running compression operation. A compatible previous
Desktop Preview 2 installation is updated using the same identity and path;
its optional desktop/Open with choices are preserved unless you change them.
Re-running this installer can repair this update.

On a new installation, desktop shortcut and Open with integration are
optional and initially off. If selected, Open with offers this application
for .gmw and .rtsf files. Your existing default apps are not changed. Opening
an archive only preselects the relevant input/screen; it does not automatically
decrypt, extract, restore, or execute it. No recovery-key association is added.

UNSIGNED WINDOWS WARNINGS

Neither Setup nor the application is Authenticode-signed. Windows, SmartScreen,
antivirus software, or an organisation's policy may warn about or block them.
Obtain the update from a trusted supplier. If blocked or uncertain, stop and
ask your administrator or support. Do not disable security protection to run
the update. A warning alone does not establish whether a download is safe.

SHA256SUMS.txt lists the other application payload files installed by Setup.
Its generated uninstaller is not included. Checksums can detect damaged or
changed files, but accompanying checksums do not prove the publisher's identity
or replace a digital signature.

TEST SAFELY AND KEEP YOUR DATA

Use generated samples first, then copies of a small test folder. Save archives
outside their source folder, restore into a different empty folder, and compare
the restored contents with the originals. Keep independent backups; do not
overwrite originals or treat a preview archive as your only backup.

Compression alone is not encryption. Encryption is optional for supported
formats, and a .gmw file is not necessarily encrypted. A live repository is not
encrypted merely because Rat-Trap created it. Keep passwords and .rtrk recovery
keys safe and separate from protected data. A matching key can unlock it;
losing the required password and recovery material can make it unrecoverable.
Restoration may produce unencrypted files.

Remove Rat-Trap Desktop Preview 2 through Windows Apps settings. Uninstall
removes its explicit installed file list, shortcuts, and registration; it does
not recursively delete its folder or remove your archives, recovery keys,
repositories, exports, restored files, other separate Rat-Trap installations,
or local diagnostics. User-created files left in its folder keep that folder
from being removed. You control retention of your data and tutorial outputs.
Local diagnostics may remain under:
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Two Hands Network Ltd\Rat-Trap\Diagnostics
