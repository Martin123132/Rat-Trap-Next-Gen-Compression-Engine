PRACTISE COMPRESSION, RESTORE, AND VAULT SEARCH

This preview adds compression and restore lessons to the existing guided
Desktop Preview 2 application. Choose Show me how, then Compress a folder,
Restore your files, or Find a file inside a Vault. Read each step before continuing. The walkthrough uses generated
sample material so you can practise the workflow before selecting personal
files. Starting a tutorial is an explicit action; merely browsing the tutorial
catalogue is not permission to process your documents.

Compression, restore, and Vault search are the three ready walkthroughs.
The compression lesson explains the source folder, archive destination,
compression choice, and measured result. The restore lesson uses a separate
sample destination and checks that recovered sample files match the originals.
Back and Exit tutorial remain available throughout. Practice work is limited
to generated sample material, not your personal files.

Protection/recovery and backup-history lessons are marked "walkthrough coming
later". Those entries do not claim a completed tutorial.

This update does not introduce new compression algorithms, archive formats,
or encryption guarantees. The established archive, protection, and restore
operations remain available. Use independent backups and copies for testing.

WINDOWS WARNINGS AND SAFE TESTING

The executable is not Authenticode-signed. Windows, SmartScreen, antivirus
software, or an organisation's policy may warn about or block it. Obtain the
preview from a trusted supplier. If blocked or unsure, stop and ask your
administrator or support; do not disable security protection to run it.

For your own first compression test, use copies in a small test folder. Save
the archive outside that folder and restore into a different empty folder.
Compare restored contents with the originals. Do not overwrite your originals
or make a preview archive your only backup. Wait for work to finish before
closing the app or disconnecting storage.

Stage text and elapsed time are not an ETA or proof that a worker is healthy.
Open archive preselects an input in the app; it does not automatically decrypt,
extract, or restore it. Show in folder opens File Explorer only on your click.

ENCRYPTION AND PRIVACY

Compression alone is not encryption. Encryption is optional for supported
formats. A .gmw file is not necessarily encrypted; a live repository is not
encrypted merely because Rat-Trap created it. Keep passwords and .rtrk recovery
keys safe and separate from protected data. A matching key can unlock that
data. Losing the required password and recovery material can make the data
unrecoverable. Restoration may produce unencrypted files.

Processing is local. Rat-Trap does not automatically upload your files or
diagnostics. Network or cloud-synchronised locations you select still follow
the chosen service's behaviour. Read PRIVACY.md for details.

UPDATES, REMOVAL, AND SUPPORT

There is no automatic updater. Close the app after work finishes before
running a compatible installer. Removing the app does not automatically remove
your archives, recovery keys, repositories, exports, restored files, or local
diagnostics. If removing a private extracted payload folder manually, first
move out any data you saved inside it.
